Thursday Bingo

State-legal charity bingo, played from a computer, every week. Some players come every Thursday, others once a month, everyone's welcome to find their rhythm.

Doors & time Doors open 4pm. Bingo begins 6pm, runs to 10pm.
Age 18 and up
Seats About 60
Food Rotating menu. Ask what's on it when you call.
House rule Kitchen food available; outside food isn't permitted.
