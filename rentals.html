Hall Rentals

A hall and a pavilion for weddings, parties, and community gatherings. Seats 150 to 160 comfortably, up to 180 at max capacity.

Why Post 4093

  • A hall and a pavilion, so you can pick the space that fits your event
  • Full kitchen and bar access for your event
  • An outdoor grill pit for pavilion gatherings
  • Run by the Post's own veterans and volunteers, right here in Carleton

How renting works

  1. 1

    Check availability

    Call or send an inquiry with your date and event type. We'll let you know what's open.

  2. 2

    Tour the space

    Stop by to see the Hall or Pavilion in person. Optional, but recommended.

  3. 3

    Reserve your date

    Sign a rental agreement to hold your date. Your date isn't reserved until this step is complete.

  4. 4

    Finalize the details

    Work out layout, timing, and any extras with a Post representative.

  5. 5

    Enjoy your event

    We're on-site to help things run smoothly on the day.
